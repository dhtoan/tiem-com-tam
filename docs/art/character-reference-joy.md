# Character Reference — Má Năm (Joy)

## Identity & Role
- **Name:** Má Năm (Joy)
- **Role:** Stall Founder, Master Cook, Head of Family
- **Age:** 46
- **Archetype:** Resilient, fiercely loving, culinary perfectionist

## Physical Description
- **Build:** Slender but sturdy from decades of kitchen labor; upright, confident posture.
- **Hair:** Dark brown/black with subtle wisps of silver at the temples; neatly tied back into a low, practical chignon bun secured with a tortoiseshell hair clip.
- **Face:** Warm brown almond eyes with distinct laughter lines and faint stress creases around the brow; prominent cheekbones, determined chin.
- **Attire:**
  - Traditional Southern Vietnamese floral or checked *áo bà ba* (cotton blouse) in earthy sage, muted coral, or soft indigo.
  - Dark comfortable trousers.
  - Heavy-duty dark cotton kitchen apron tied firmly at the waist with front utility pockets (for tasting spoon, towel, pencil).
  - Simple gold stud earrings, jade bangle on left wrist (family heirloom).

## Emotional Expressions & Key Visual States
1. **`joy_neutral`:** Calm, focused concentration; attentive gaze toward customers or cutting board.
2. **`joy_happy`:** Warm, radiant smile, eyes crinkling with pride when serving a perfect plate or praising JD.
3. **`joy_worried`:** Tightened lips, furrowed brow, looking down at ledger balance or debt collection notice.
4. **`joy_determined`:** Stern, focused glare with sleeves rolled up, facing health inspections, tough weather, or aggressive debt milestones.
