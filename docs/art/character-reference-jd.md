# Character Reference — JD (Học Việc / Trợ Thủ)

## Identity & Role
- **Name:** JD (Gia Định / Tiến)
- **Role:** Stall Assistant, Joy's Nephew & Apprentice
- **Age:** 18
- **Archetype:** Earnest, loyal, quick learner, eager to prove himself

## Physical Description
- **Build:** Youthful, energetic, lean and wiry; moves with agility and enthusiasm.
- **Hair:** Modern textured black crop, slightly tousled from rushing between the grill and dining tables.
- **Face:** Bright, open, expressive almond eyes; boyish round chin; lively smile; honest, transparent emotions.
- **Attire:**
  - Crisp plain white or navy oversized crewneck T-shirt.
  - Rolled-up dark denim or khaki utility pants, clean sneakers.
  - Short waist-tie apron in dark tan or denim blue with a grease pencil tucked behind his ear.
  - A woven braided red string wristband on his right arm.

## Emotional Expressions & Key Visual States
1. **`jd_neutral`:** Attentive, hands ready, waiting for Má Năm's command or looking out for incoming patrons.
2. **`jd_proud`:** Standing tall with chest puffed out, holding up a freshly cleaned tray or successful prep batch.
3. **`jd_nervous`:** Hand behind his neck, sheepish grin after a near-mishap or when customer queue builds up.
4. **`jd_tired`:** Slumped shoulders, apron slightly dusted in flour/ash after long double-shifts, wiping sweat from forehead with his forearm.
