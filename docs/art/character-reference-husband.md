# Character Reference — Ba Long (Husband)

## Identity & Role
- **Name:** Ba Long
- **Role:** Joy's Husband, Former Workshop Craftsman, Story Catalyst & Moral Pillar
- **Age:** 50
- **Archetype:** Thoughtful, recovering from business setback, deeply devoted father/husband

## Physical Description
- **Build:** Tall, broad-shouldered craftsman frame, slightly stooped in early days from carrying guilt, growing taller and prouder as the family rebuilds.
- **Hair:** Dark peppered with silver grey along the sides and temples, neatly combed to the side.
- **Face:** Gentle, weathered features; kind, patient dark eyes with crow's feet; subtle five-o'clock shadow or clean-shaven.
- **Attire:**
  - **Early Campaign (Days 1–24):** Muted grey or olive collared polo shirt, dark slacks, leather sandals. Often seen in background holding household paperwork or repairing stall fixtures.
  - **Late Campaign (Days 25–30 & Payoff):** Wearing a matching canvas chef's apron alongside Má Năm at the grill, sleeves rolled up to the elbows, tongs in hand with charcoal smudge on forearm.

## Emotional Expressions & Key Visual States
1. **`husband_neutral`:** Thoughtful, quiet contemplation; supportive half-smile as he watches the stall operate.
2. **`husband_guilty`:** Downcast eyes, shoulders pulled inward, clutching financial statement or medicine box.
3. **`husband_apron`:** Radiant, proud smile, standing shoulder-to-shoulder with Má Năm in front of the sizzling grill station.
4. **`husband_relieved`:** Soft, emotional tears of joy upon final debt settlement or family reunion.
